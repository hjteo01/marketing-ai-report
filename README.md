# Marketing AI

## Problem

During my internship at the **National Library Board (NLB)**, I built a **Tableau dashboard** to track email campaign performance. While the dashboard showed which campaigns performed well or poorly, marketers still had to manually figure out **why campaigns worked** and **how future campaigns could be improved**.

This made campaign optimisation **time-consuming** and **difficult to scale**.

## Solution

I developed **Marketing AI**, an **AI-powered platform** that turns email campaign performance data into actionable recommendations.

The platform analyses **campaign metrics**, **content themes**, and **messaging patterns** to identify what resonates with audiences. Using **Google Gemini**, it generates recommendations across four categories:

- **Continue** – what is already working well
- **Amplify** – strong content with more potential
- **Boost** – campaigns that can be improved
- **Deprioritise** – approaches to avoid

Built with **HTML**, **JavaScript**, **ECharts**, and **Gemini AI**.

## Outcome

**Marketing AI helps marketers move from simply understanding what happened to knowing what to do next.**

Instead of spending hours manually reviewing campaign results, marketers can quickly identify successful content patterns and use **AI-generated recommendations** to improve future campaign strategy.
