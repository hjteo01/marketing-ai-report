# Marketing AI

## Problem

The existing **Tableau email campaign dashboard** provided visibility into campaign performance metrics such as **opens, clicks, and engagement rates**. While marketers could identify which campaigns performed well or poorly, the dashboard could not explain **why** certain campaigns succeeded or provide guidance on how future campaigns should be improved.

As a result, campaign optimisation relied heavily on manual analysis of **content themes**, **messaging patterns**, and **audience behaviour**, making the process time-consuming and difficult to scale.

## Solution

Developed an **AI-powered marketing intelligence platform** that combines campaign performance analytics with **Large Language Model (LLM)** capabilities.

Using historical email campaign data, the platform classifies campaigns into performance segments and analyses **content themes**, **messaging patterns**, and **audience engagement metrics**. **Google Gemini** is then used to generate campaign-specific insights and recommendations across four strategic categories:

- **Continue** – High-performing campaigns to replicate
- **Amplify** – Strong content with untapped potential
- **Boost** – Campaigns requiring optimisation
- **Deprioritise** – Low-performing campaigns to avoid

Built an interactive web application using **HTML**, **JavaScript**, **ECharts**, and **Gemini AI** to deliver campaign analytics and AI-generated recommendations in a single interface.

## Outcome

Transformed campaign reporting from a **descriptive analytics dashboard** into an **AI-assisted decision-support tool** for marketers.

The platform reduces the manual effort required to analyse campaign performance while providing actionable recommendations to improve future content strategy, helping marketing teams optimise **audience reach**, **engagement**, and **conversion potential**.
