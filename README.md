# Marketing AI

## Problem

During my internship at the **National Library Board (NLB)**, I built a **Tableau dashboard** to track email campaign performance through metrics such as **open rates**, **click rates**, and **engagement levels**. While the dashboard helped marketers understand which campaigns performed well or poorly, it did not explain the reasons behind the results or suggest how future campaigns could be improved.

As a result, marketers still had to manually review campaign content, identify performance patterns, and develop recommendations themselves, making the optimisation process **time-consuming** and **difficult to scale**.

## Solution

To address this gap, I developed **Marketing AI**, an **AI-powered platform** that transforms campaign performance data into actionable recommendations.

Using historical email campaign data, the platform analyses **engagement metrics**, **content themes**, and **messaging patterns** to identify what resonates with audiences. Leveraging **Google Gemini**, it automatically generates recommendations across four categories:

- **Continue** – Content strategies that are already performing well
- **Amplify** – High-potential content that deserves greater exposure
- **Boost** – Campaigns that can be improved through optimisation
- **Deprioritise** – Low-performing approaches that should be avoided

The solution was built using **HTML**, **JavaScript**, **ECharts**, and **Gemini AI**, providing marketers with an interactive interface to explore campaign performance and AI-generated insights.

## Outcome

**Marketing AI transforms campaign reporting from a tool that explains what happened into one that suggests what to do next.**

Instead of spending hours manually reviewing campaign results, marketers can quickly identify successful content patterns and receive **AI-generated recommendations** to improve future campaigns. This supports better decision-making around **audience engagement**, **content strategy**, and **campaign planning**.
